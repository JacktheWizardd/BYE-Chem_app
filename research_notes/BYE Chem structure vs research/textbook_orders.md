# Chapter orders and chapter structure of major general-chemistry textbooks vs BYE Chem

Scope note: chapter titles fetched from publisher/OpenStax source pages unless marked UNVERIFIED. Only titles and structure recorded. Tro 6e (Molecular Approach) is the already-known reference and not re-researched.

## 1. Chapter order of each book (edition stated for each)

### Takeaway
Six of eight target books were retrieved with full chapter lists from primary sources (OpenStax 2e, OpenStax Atoms First 2e, Brown 15e, Zumdahl 10e, Silberberg 10e, Tro Structure & Properties 3e). Chang/Overby 14e and Burdge/Overby Atoms First 5e could NOT be fully verified (only fragments). All verified books put quantum/electron structure BEFORE Lewis/VSEPR bonding, and none gives the mole its own chapter.

## 3. Unusual positions in the BYE Chem order vs published books

### Takeaway
Most-unusual choices: Bonds/VSEPR (ch 9) before quantum (ch 10); mole as a standalone chapter; Solutions (14) before Solids and Materials (15). No verified book does any of these exactly.

### Cited Findings / Comparison (BYE position -> published precedent among verified books)
- **Lewis/VSEPR before quantum** — Not found in any verified book. Every verified book (OpenStax 2e & AF, Brown, Zumdahl, Silberberg, Tro S&P, Burdge ch 3 snippet) places quantum/electronic structure before Lewis/VSEPR. Even atoms-first books do atoms -> quantum -> periodicity -> bonding.
- **Mole as its own chapter (Ch 4)** — Not found. Closest: OpenStax 2e ch 3 and AF ch 6 "Composition of Substances and Solutions" (mole/molar mass/formula composition + solution concentration in one chapter, separate from reaction stoichiometry). Tro S&P splits mole between ch 4 and ch 7. All others fold the mole into the stoichiometry chapter (Brown 3, Zumdahl 3, Silberberg 3).
- **Reactions in Water (aqueous: molarity, precipitation, acid–base, redox) as one chapter after stoichiometry** — Standard: Brown 4, Zumdahl 4, Silberberg 4 "Three Major Classes of Chemical Reactions", Tro S&P 8.
- **Gases (7) before Thermochemistry (8)** — Matches Zumdahl, Silberberg, OpenStax AF; reverse of Brown, OpenStax 2e, Tro.
- **Thermochemistry placed BEFORE quantum, with bonds next** — Matches traditional books (thermo before quantum) but their bonding comes after quantum.
- **Why Bonds Form (lattice/bond energy, VB, MO) separate from VSEPR (ch 12 vs 9)** — Traditional books combine "bonding theories" as a pair of chapters after the periodic chapter: Brown 8/9, Zumdahl 8/9, OpenStax 7/8, Tro S&P 5/6. BYE inserts quantum + periodic table between them (ch 10-11) — no verified precedent.
- **Liquids/Solids/IMFs (13) -> Solutions (14) -> Solids and Materials (15)** — Published order is liquids/IMF -> solids -> solutions in Brown (11,12,13) and Tro S&P (11,12,13); Silberberg and OpenStax 2e have one Liquids & Solids chapter then Solutions. Solutions before a solids/materials chapter has no verified precedent.
- **Organic (16) before kinetics (17)** — Silberberg ch 15 organic before kinetics 16 is the only verified analogue; others put organic at/near the end.
- **Kinetics -> Equilibrium -> Acids and Bases -> Buffers/Titrations/Solubility -> Entropy/Free Energy -> Electrochemistry -> Nuclear** — Very close to Brown/Tro/Silberberg tail (Tro S&P puts thermodynamics after aqueous equilibria, electro, nuclear, organic, TM). Thermodynamics (BYE 21) after aqueous equilibria matches all; Zumdahl also puts nuclear after electrochemistry.
- **Math chapter 0 / separate prerequisite maths** — Closest: Tro S&P "Essentials: Units, Measurement, and Problem Solving" unit plus Appendix I "Common Mathematical Operations in Chemistry" ([Pearson](https://www.pearson.com/en-us/subject-catalog/p/chemistry-structure-and-properties/P200000009744/9780138095505)); Silberberg ch 1 includes "Problem Solving" in its title. Math is otherwise within ch 1 (OpenStax 1.6 "Mathematical Treatment of Measurement Results" — [openstax.org](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/1-introduction)).
- **First pH look inside aqueous chapter** — Plausible but UNVERIFIED for all books; no title evidence retrieved.

### Inferences
- The two largest departures from consensus are bonds-before-quantum and the standalone mole chapter; both are defensible pedagogy (octet-rule shapes need only group number) but have no verified textbook precedent here. Reordering risk: students/teachers matching to a standard text will see Lewis/VSEPR in a different place than any listed book.
- Nonmetals/metals/transition metals at the end (24-25) is standard (Brown 22-23, Zumdahl 20-21, Silberberg 22-23, OpenStax 18-19).

### Gaps
- Whether any smaller-market book (not in this list) does bonds before quantum; not searched.

## 4. How the books structure a chapter internally

### Takeaway
Only OpenStax features were checked on live pages (and only partly); all other books' features below are UNVERIFIED from background knowledge, since no primary page describing them was retrieved. Common pattern: chapter opener/objectives, worked examples with a solution then a "practice/Check Your Learning", summary, end-of-chapter exercises.

### Cited Findings
- **OpenStax Chemistry 2e section pages:** each section starts with a "Learning Objectives" bulleted list (5 objectives on 1.1; 3 on 4.3); figures with captions and credits; worked examples are numbered (e.g., Examples 4.8-4.11) with a bold title, problem statement, "Solution" heading, then "Check Your Learning" with an "Answer"; a sidebar "Chemistry in Everyday Life" box (e.g., "Airbags") — [1.1](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/1-1-chemistry-in-context), [4.3](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/4-3-reaction-stoichiometry). Not observed on the 1.1 page: Link to Learning, Key Equations, Summary, Exercises (those live on separate chapter-end pages; presence at chapter end is UNVERIFIED here).
- **OpenStax chapter opener:** chapter intro page followed by numbered sections (confirmed ch 1 has six sections) — [ch1 intro](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/1-introduction).
- **Math support:** Tro S&P has appendix "Common Mathematical Operations in Chemistry" and an opening "Essentials" unit — [Pearson](https://www.pearson.com/en-us/subject-catalog/p/chemistry-structure-and-properties/P200000009744/9780138095505); has appendices "Answers to In-Chapter Practice Problems" and "Answers to Selected End-of-Chapter Problems" (same source). OpenStax 2e ch 1 ends with a section on mathematical treatment of measurements (dimensional analysis/units) — same ch 1 TOC.
- **Silberberg:** online-only "Advanced Topics" chapters 7A, 16A, 20A — [McGraw Hill](https://www.mheducation.com/highered/product/chemistry-the-molecular-nature-of-matter-and-change-silberberg.html).
- **Brown:** chapters carry the "Chemistry Put to Work"/"Strategies in Chemistry" boxes, "Sample Exercise" with Analyze/Plan/Solve/Check and "Practice Exercise", "Chapter Summary and Key Terms", "Learning Outcomes", "Key Equations" — UNVERIFIED (background knowledge, not fetched).
- **Tro:** "Sample Problem/Example" with Sort/Strategize/Solve/Check, "For Practice"/"For More Practice", "Conceptual Connection", Chapter Summary with Key Terms, Key Concepts, Key Equations and Relationships, Key Skills, Exercises — UNVERIFIED (background knowledge). Tro 6e structure was not to be re-researched.
- **Zumdahl, Chang/Overby, Burdge/Overby:** feature sets (Zumdahl: "Interactive Example", "Sample Exercise", "Self Check", "Critical Thinking"; Chang: "Worked Example" with Strategy/Setup/Solution/Think About It and "Practice Problem", Key Equations; Burdge: similar to Chang) — UNVERIFIED, not fetched.

### Inferences
- Solve/Check-style multi-step worked-example frames (Brown's Analyze/Plan/Solve/Check; Tro's Sort/Strategize/Solve/Check; OpenStax's Solution -> Check Your Learning) are common; the dimension that differs is whether the "Check" is a sanity-check step (Brown/Tro/Chang) vs a separate practice problem (OpenStax). This is based on partly unverified background knowledge.

### Gaps
- No primary page retrieved describing Brown, Zumdahl, Silberberg, Chang, Burdge, or Tro chapter features; opening hooks/stories not verified for any book; OpenStax chapter-end Summary/Key Equations/Exercises pages not fetched. Explicit "math toolbox" or prerequisite-review features beyond Tro's Essentials/Appendix I and OpenStax 1.6 were not found.
