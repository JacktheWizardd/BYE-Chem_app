## 2. CHEM 001A (official catalog entry)

Source for all of this section: https://curriculum.pasadena.edu/course-descriptions/chem/chem.pdf

- Title: General Chemistry and Chemical Analysis I. Units: 5.
- Hours: 54 hours lecture + 108 hours laboratory (total per term).
- Transfer: CSU; UC with credit limitations. Grade mode: letter grade or pass/no-pass. C-ID: CHEM 110, and CHEM SEQ 120S (together with 001B).
- Prerequisites: (1) Intermediate Algebra, or placement into any MATH course numbered 001-099, and (2) CHEM 022, or equivalent skills shown through the chemistry assessment process. ## 3. CHEM 001B

Source: https://curriculum.pasadena.edu/course-descriptions/chem/chem.pdf
- General Chemistry and Chemical Analysis II, 5 units, 54 lecture + 108 lab hours. Prerequisite: CHEM 001A. C-ID: CHEM SEQ 120S (with 001A). CSU; UC with limitations.
- Description (paraphrase): second half of the standard general chemistry sequence. Kinetics, equilibrium, thermodynamics, intro to electrochemistry, coordination compounds, nuclear chemistry, chemistry of selected metals and nonmetals; potentiometric titrations and electrochemical cells in lab.
- Maps (inference) to OpenStax ch 12-17, 19, 21 (and ch 18 for metals/nonmetals).

## 5. C-ID and transfer

- **C-ID CHEM 110, "General Chemistry for Science Majors I, with Lab"**, 5 units, revised 2022-06-07. Source: https://data-c-idsystem.org/descriptors/final/show/297
  - Prerequisites (descriptor): CHEM 101 and intermediate algebra or higher / eligibility for transfer-level math.
  - Required content (the first semester must include): nomenclature; atomic structure; basic quantum theory and electronic structure; periodic properties; chemical reactions; stoichiometry; gas laws; molecular structure and bonding; states of matter; aqueous solutions. Topics may be revisited in semester two.
  - Lab: mostly hands-on quantitative work; examples include safety training, solution preparation, measurements, titration, gravimetric analysis, spectrophotometry. At least 1 unit of lab.
  - Suggested textbooks listed in the descriptor include Tro, Brown et al. (Central Science), McMurry and Fay, Chang and Overby, Atkins and Jones.
  - Thermochemistry is NOT in the required content list (so PCC's calorimetry/energy topics go beyond the minimum).
- **C-ID CHEM SEQ 120S** = CHEM 110 + CHEM 120 (the two-semester sequence PCC 001A + 001B map to). Descriptor text for 120S/CHEM 120 itself: not fetched, UNVERIFIED (only PCC's catalog statement of the pairing).
- **C-ID CHEM 101 "Introduction to Chemistry"** (PCC CHEM 022 and 002A map here), 4 units, revised 2022-06-07. Source: https://data-c-idsystem.org/descriptors/final/show/432
  - Prerequisite: elementary algebra or higher / eligibility for transfer-level math. Content: matter (atoms and elements); nomenclature; measurement; chemical bonds; molecular structure; chemical reactions; stoichiometry; energy and states of matter; gas laws; aqueous systems; solutions; acids, bases and salts. Outcomes include mass/volume/density/pressure/temperature conversions, solution concentrations and dilutions, naming and writing formulas, acid/base behaviour, theoretical yield. Textbooks listed: Tro, Introductory Chemistry; Zumdahl, Introduction to Chemistry.
  - Note: C-ID says elementary algebra; PCC requires intermediate algebra for CHEM 022 (PCC is stricter).
- **ASSIST.org (UC articulation of PCC CHEM 001A)**: UNVERIFIED. ASSIST is a JavaScript app and its API refused plain requests, and web search surfaced no UCLA/UC Berkeley agreement text. PCC states ASSIST is the official articulation source: https://curriculum.pasadena.edu/transfer-information/assist/ . Manual check: assist.org, sending school PCC, receiving UCLA/UC Berkeley, search CHEM 001A, pick the right catalog year (UCSC notes the year matters: https://sites.google.com/ucsc.edu/chemistryadvising/transfer-credit ). The catalog entry itself says "UC credit limitations. See counselor."

## 7. Zero-to-1A path for an adult with no background

Grounded in PCC's prerequisite chain (CHEM 022 -> 001A -> 001B, https://curriculum.pasadena.edu/course-descriptions/chem/chem.pdf) and the C-ID CHEM 101 descriptor (https://data-c-idsystem.org/descriptors/final/show/432), which is the statewide definition of the intro course.

Stage 0, math floor. PCC wants intermediate algebra (or placement) for both CHEM 022 and 001A; C-ID 101 wants elementary algebra (same sources). The specific algebra skills are not listed in either source (UNVERIFIED). Likely needed (inference, from the nature of the topics): arithmetic with fractions/decimals, percent, ratios and unit conversion, scientific notation, solving one-variable equations, rearranging formulas, exponents, and later logarithms (pH) and quadratics (equilibrium) in 001A/001B.

Stage 1, "Intro chemistry" (C-ID CHEM 101 content list, in the descriptor's order):
1. Matter: atoms and elements
2. Nomenclature
3. Measurement (units, conversions, density, temperature, pressure)
4. Chemical bonds
5. Molecular structure
6. Chemical reactions
7. Stoichiometry (including theoretical yield)
8. Energy and states of matter
9. Gas laws
10. Aqueous systems
11. Solutions (concentration, dilution)
12. Acids, bases and salts

Stage 2, bridge to 1A: everything in the C-ID CHEM 110 list repeats at greater depth with quantum theory/electron configuration new; see section 5 and 2.

Suggested BYE Chem entry order (my proposal, not official): ch 1 -> 2 -> 3 -> 4 first (the shared core of both courses), then ch 9 (gases), ch 7, ch 6, ch 11, ch 5, with ch 14.1-14.2 (acids/pH) as a pre-taste, since both C-ID 101 lists acids/bases and 001A's later equilibrium intro needs it.
