# M. Tro 6e: chapter and section numbers

Source: Nivaldo J. Tro, *Chemistry: A Molecular Approach*, AP Edition, 6th ed. (Pearson, 2022; ISBN 978-0-13-765781-0), Contents pp. xi–xxii. This is the book the friend's class uses.

**Numbers and pages only.** The book's own chapter and section titles are not reproduced here, because this repository is public and the book is under copyright. Look them up in the book. BYE Chem's own titles for each section are in `docs/chapter-map.md`.

How this list was made (2026-11-07):
- The contents pages (PDF pages 12–24) were rendered to images and transcribed.
- Chapters 1, 5, 6, 7, 17 and 18 were checked against the page images.
- The book has 26 chapters and 229 sections. Section x.1 in each chapter is an opening story. The front matter has no AP-unit mapping.

**Use:** for section references and coverage only. Never copy the book's text, examples, exercises or figures.
