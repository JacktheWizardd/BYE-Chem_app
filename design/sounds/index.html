Pick a sound set

Three sets of real lab recordings. Each set has tap (buttons and rows), select (tabs, choosing an item) and done (the end of a worked example). They have the same length and loudness as BYE Math's sounds.

In the app, sounds play at 7 % volume and only after the learner turns Sounds on. Full volume is easier for comparing.
