BYE Chem: chapters, lab and tools

A standalone app with the BYE Math structure you already know. Home lists numbered chapters; a chapter page tells the story, maps its sections and checks you’re ready; each section has Learn, then Simple, Medium and Hard. Worked problems are on hold, so this round is the chapters, plus a Lab tab (sandboxes to play in) and a Tools tab (look-ups you use every day).

Chapters follow our own beginner order: maths first, the mole as its own chapter, bonds and shapes before quantum. Each chapter and section says where it is in Tro, Chemistry: A Molecular Approach 6e, the class book. Tags show which course needs a chapter: Intro (a first chemistry course, like PCC CHEM 022), Chem 1A (like PCC CHEM 001A), Chem 1B, and AP. The two Lab sandboxes, the molar-mass calculator and the periodic table below actually work: try them.

Chapter 4 · §4.1
The Mole and Molar Mass
モルとモル質量
In your book: Tro 2.9, 3.8
LearnSimpleMediumHard

You can’t count atoms one by one, so you count them by weighing. One mole of anything is 6.022 × 1023 of it, the way one dozen is 12.

The tool
moles = grams ÷ molar mass
Key ideas4
1
Formula mass
Add the masses of every atom in the formula. H2O: 2 × 1.008 + 16.00 = 18.02.
2
The mole is a count
6.022 × 1023 particles, chosen so the numbers line up with grams.
3
Molar mass
The mass of one mole: same number as the formula mass, in grams. Water: 18.02 g/mol.
4
Count by weighing
Divide grams by molar mass and you have moles, then particles.
